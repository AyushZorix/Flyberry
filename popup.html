FlightBerry

Watched routes are re-checked about every 3 hours while your browser is open. You get a notification when a price drops or reaches your target.

Prices by seller

Without a key, prices come from Kiwi.com. Add a free Google Flights data key and each flight also shows what MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip, ixigo, the airline and other sellers charge, with the lowest marked.

  1. Create a free account at serpapi.com.
  2. Copy "Your Private API Key" from its dashboard.
  3. Paste it below and press Save.

The free plan allows 250 searches a month. Each flight search here uses 2 (3 for a return trip), and each extra "Compare sellers" click uses 1 (2 for a return trip). The key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to serpapi.com.
